PROLOGUE / 序 ANATOMY OF A HUNGER

THE SEVEN ARE NOT MONSTERS.
THEY ARE MIRRORS WITH GOOD LIGHTING.

七宗罪不是七只怪兽。
它们是七面镜子，恰好打着光。

We call them sins when we mean the appetites that run us. This is not a courtroom. It is a field guide: seven conditions, seven instruments, one human being trying them on.

我们把它们叫作罪，往往只是在说那些支配我们的渴望。这里不是法庭，而是一册观察手册：七种状态，七件仪器，以及一个正在试穿它们的人。

I

PRIDE 傲慢

SUPERBIA THE SIN OF THE MIRROR
镜中之罪

THE EYE
THAT FEEDS
ON BEING
SEEN.

傲慢不是大声说话。它是把每一段对话，悄悄转回自己。

Pride believes the self is a gallery with no exit — and every visitor an audience. It is not confidence. It is a need that has learned to wear good clothes.

它把自我变成一间没有出口的美术馆，把每个来客都当作观众。傲慢不是自信，而是一种学会了穿好衣服的需要。

NOTE 01

The opposite of pride is not self-erasure. It is scale.
傲慢的反面不是自我抹除，而是尺度。

II

GREED 贪婪

AVARITIA THE SIN OF THE CALCULATION
计算之罪

ENOUGH
IS A MOVING
TARGET.

贪婪不是想要东西。它想要的是“再多一点”的那个瞬间。

Greed never arrives. It is the arithmetic of the almost. The thing is only a receipt for the feeling it promised.

贪婪从不抵达。它是“差一点”的算术。东西只是收据，证明某种感觉曾经被许诺。

NOTE 02

A number has no horizon.
数字没有地平线。

III

LUST 色欲

LUXURIA THE SIN OF PROXIMITY
靠近之罪

THE BODY
SPEAKS
FIRST.

欲望总在解释之前抵达。

Lust is not only about bodies. It is the ache to be chosen, to be held briefly inside someone else's attention. A pulse is a poor witness, but an honest one.

色欲不只关于身体。它是被选中的渴望，是在另一个人的注意力里短暂停留的渴望。脉搏不是好证人，但它从不说谎。

NOTE 03

To want is to admit there is room for something else.
渴望，就是承认心里还有余地。

NEXT / 下一章 IV · ENVY ↘
IV

ENVY 嫉妒

INVIDIA THE SIN OF THE OTHER
他者之罪

SOMEONE
ELSE IS LIVING
YOUR LIFE
BETTER.

嫉妒是把自己的生活，交给别人的坐标。

Envy does not take what you have. It edits the story you tell about it. It is the green arithmetic of a life measured in someone else's light.

嫉妒不拿走你拥有的东西。它改写的，是你关于这些东西的故事。它是用别人的光来丈量自己的绿色算术。

NOTE 04

Comparison is a room with two mirrors and no door.
比较是一间有两面镜子、却没有门的房间。

NEXT / 下一章 V · GLUTTONY ↘
V

GLUTTONY 暴食

GULA THE SIN OF THE INTAKE
摄入之罪

A MOUTH
WITH NO
FLOOR.

暴食不是饥饿，是填满的冲动。

Gluttony is not appetite. It is appetite with a hole underneath. We feed it information, objects, affection, noise — and call the silence afterwards full.

暴食不是食欲，是底下有洞的食欲。我们喂它信息、物品、爱意与噪音，然后把之后的沉默叫作“饱”。

NOTE 05

A mouth will accept almost anything except enough.
一张嘴几乎什么都接受，除了“够了”。

NEXT / 下一章 VI · WRATH ↘
VI

WRATH 暴怒

IRA THE SIN OF THE WOUND
伤口之罪

HURT
IS A
DIRECTION.

暴怒是疼痛穿上了盔甲。

Wrath is pain in armor. It needs a target because pain without one is just weather inside the body. Sometimes it protects. Sometimes it only makes more weather.

暴怒是穿上盔甲的疼痛。它需要一个目标，因为没有目标的疼痛只能在身体里变成天气。它有时保护你，有时只是制造更多天气。

NOTE 06

Heat always looks for a direction.
热度总要找一个方向。

NEXT / 下一章 VII · SLOTH ↘
VII

SLOTH 怠惰

ACEDIA THE SIN OF THE PAUSE
停顿之罪

THE WORLD
CAN WAIT.

怠惰有时不是懒，是一场安静的罢工。

Sloth is not always laziness. Sometimes it is a strike against a world in which everything must be useful. To pause is to let the soul catch up with the body.

怠惰不总是懒惰。有时它是对“一切必须有用”的世界的罢工。停下来，是让灵魂追上身体。

NOTE 07

The clock is not a moral instrument.
时钟不是道德仪器。

NEXT / 下一章 THE CONFESSIONAL ↘
VIII

THE CONFESSIONAL 告解室

PECCAVIMUS THE ROOM AFTER THE ROOMS
所有房间之后

NOBODY
GETS OUT
CLEAN.

不是因为你肮脏，是因为你活着。

Not because you are guilty — because you are alive. The seven are not a verdict. They are a map of the places you have already been. You don't have to choose one; you only have to admit they all know you.

不是因为你肮脏，而是因为你活着。七宗罪不是判决，而是一张你已经走过的地方的地图。你不必从中挑选一个身份；你只需要承认，它们都认识你。
